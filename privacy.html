Privacy Policy

Before launch, replace this preview with the operator's complete privacy policy and contact details, reviewed for applicable laws.

Account information

When configured, Firebase Authentication processes the email address, account name, password credential, authentication state, and verification status needed to create and secure Bite Lab accounts. Firebase manages password credentials and sends email verification and password-reset links; Bite Lab does not store passwords in its own application database.

Verification and password reset

Firebase sends verification and password-reset links to the account email address when requested. Google and Apple may process information under their own policies when their sign-in providers are enabled and chosen.

Storage and service providers

Firebase web app configuration identifies the public Firebase project and is included in the website. Private service-account credentials must not be included in frontend code. Account data is processed by Google Firebase under its service terms and privacy policy.
