Privacy Policy

This preview is a static informational website. It does not provide user accounts, sign-in, contact forms, mailing lists, analytics, or payment processing and does not use cookies for tracking.

Information collected by hosting

When you visit the site, GitHub Pages may process standard technical request information, such as your IP address, browser details, and requested page, to operate and protect its hosting service. GitHub's privacy policy describes how GitHub handles that information.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's font service to retrieve those fonts; Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Changes and contact

This policy applies to the current preview and may change if the site adds interactive features. A monitored contact address will be added if the site begins collecting information.
